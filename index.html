What this page does

Your weather station sends one reading every hour.

How it works, step by step

  1. Collect the hourly readings.
  2. Work out what was normal for that hour.
  3. Compare the reading with that range.
  4. Check the simple physical rules.
  5. Group similar hours.
  6. Explain every flag in words.

Good to know

  • One reading per hour.
  • The record is one week long.
  • A single unusual value is only a hint.
  • Rule descriptions stay in English.
  • No hardware and no login.
